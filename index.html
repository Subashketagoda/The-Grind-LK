THE GRIND PHILOSOPHY

Where Specialty Coffee Meets Community

Nestled on Dharmapala Mawatha, The Grind is Colombo's haven for specialty coffee enthusiasts. We obsess over every detail: from sourcing single-origin beans at origin to calibrating grinder burrs daily and steaming milk to microfoam perfection.

Micro-Batch Roasting

Carefully profiled beans from Myanmar, Ethiopia, and Colombia roasted to highlight delicate terroir.

Handmade Artisan Bakery

Authentic boiled bagels, flaky treats, and gourmet brunch bowls prepared fresh daily.
